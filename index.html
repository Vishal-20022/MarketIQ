Data scope, quality and metric definitions

Net revenue is quantity × unit price for non-cancellation invoices with positive quantity and price. Cancellation invoices and non-positive lines are excluded. Orders are unique completed invoice numbers. Average order value is net revenue divided by completed orders. Customer metrics use rows with an identified CustomerID. RFM recency is measured from the latest eligible transaction in the selected date range; frequency counts unique invoices; monetary value is net revenue.
